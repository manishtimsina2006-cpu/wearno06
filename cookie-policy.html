Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This policy explains how NO.06 uses cookies and similar technology on wearno06.com, and how you can control them.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a site stores on your device. They let a site remember who you are, what you’ve done, and how you got there.

2. What we use them for

  • Essential: keeping the site working, like processing your order and remembering items in checkout.
  • Analytics: understanding how people use the site, through basic technical data such as browser type, device and pages visited.
  • Marketing: measuring and improving our early access and drop emails.

We don’t use cookies to sell your personal information.

3. Third-party cookies

Some cookies are set by the services that help us run the store, such as Stripe (payments), our website host, and our form and email providers. Each is governed by that provider’s own policy.

4. Your choices

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies in their settings. Blocking essential cookies may stop parts of the site, like checkout, from working properly.

5. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
